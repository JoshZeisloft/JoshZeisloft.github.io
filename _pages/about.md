I am currently an Automation Engineer at Adient, an automotive seating manufacturer, where I design, integrate, and troubleshoot robotic automation systems used in high-volume manufacturing environments. I graduated from the Georgia Institute of Technology in 2022 with a degree in Mechanical Engineering and a minor in Robotics. I am currently based in Ann Arbor, Michigan, and am looking to continue developing my career in robotic automation with an emphasis on mechanical design, systems engineering, and the development of complete automation solutions.

I have been working with industrial robotics since 2017, beginning with an internship at a robotic integration company where I developed robotic demonstrations and gained early experience designing and integrating automated systems. Since then, I have continued building experience across the full automation lifecycle, including mechanical design, robotic programming, system integration, commissioning, troubleshooting, and production support.

What I'm looking for
======
I am seeking a full-time Mechanical Designer position where I can combine my mechanical engineering background with my experience in robotic automation. I am particularly interested in developing mechanical concepts and layouts for robotic cells, designing equipment and assemblies, and working through the complete process from initial concept to design, build, integration, installation, and testing.

I am particularly interested in combining my interests of mechanical design, robotic systems engineering and customer applications. I would bring a strong foundation in mechanical engineering, industrial robotics experience, hands-on automation knowledge, and a genuine interest in designing reliable robotic systems. I am eager to continue developing my skills while contributing to deliver innovative, practical, and dependable automation solutions to customers.

What I can offer
======
My experience in automotive manufacturing has given me a strong understanding of the demands placed on industrial automation. I have experience designing and working with robotic cells where reliability, safety, maintainability, cycle time, and repeatability are critical. I approach mechanical and automation problems with the goal of developing solutions that are not only functional, but also practical to build, operate, troubleshoot, and maintain.

My mechanical engineering background provides a strong foundation in mechanical design, machine components, assemblies, and engineering principles. Combined with my robotics experience, this allows me to consider the complete system rather than viewing mechanical design and robot integration as separate problems. I am comfortable working through design challenges with the end application in mind and refining solutions based on testing and real-world performance.

I also bring hands-on troubleshooting and integration experience. Working in automotive manufacturing has required me to identify problems, determine their root causes, and develop solutions while working around production requirements and deadlines. I enjoy the iterative nature of automation engineering—designing a system, building it, testing it, identifying shortcomings, and improving it until it performs as intended.

Collaboration is another important part of my engineering approach. Robotic automation projects require close coordination between mechanical, electrical, controls, programming, manufacturing, and customer teams. I am comfortable communicating technical information across disciplines and working with others to resolve design and integration issues efficiently.
